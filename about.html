OUR STORY

Welcome to Trekkers Hub

Trekkers Hub is a community of passionate trekkers and outdoor enthusiasts dedicated to exploring the majestic Sahyadri mountain ranges across Maharashtra.

We organize trekking trips to historic forts, waterfalls, misty peaks, and scenic nature trails. Our mission is to make outdoor adventures safe, affordable, and accessible for everyone — from beginners to seasoned hikers.

Every trek is led by experienced trek leaders who ensure proper safety, guidance, and an unforgettable journey in nature.
